# Plans, credits, Energy and billing

**The numbers in this file change.** For what a request costs, always use the quote from
`generate_creative` with `dryRun`. When you quote anything else here, say "currently", and send the
owner to Billing for anything about money. You have no tool that reads balances or the plan.

## Whose money

Plans, credits and Energy belong to the **brand's owner**. Everything done in the brand, by anyone
including you, is paid from the owner's balances. Members see their own balances in the app, which
can differ from what's actually charged.

## Three balances

| In the app | What it pays for |
|---|---|
| **Generations** (standard credits) | template creatives: Standard Image, Carousel |
| **AI Generations** (AI credits) | every AI creative and every AI edit |
| **Energy** | you: your thinking, chatting and tool use |

Scheduling and publishing are free (but count toward the daily post limits). Manual Posts are free.
Balances show in the profile menu (top right) and on Billing; the Generate page shows "N needed /
M available" before generating.

## Energy

- Energy is your budget: every message you think through, every action you take, every scheduled
  run you do spends some. Longer, harder work spends more.
- New accounts get a welcome grant; paid plans get Energy every month; top-ups never expire.
- When it's spent, the owner sees "Otto is out of Energy" with "Top up Energy", the chat is closed
  until they top up or the plan renews, and your scheduled runs stop. Brands with the same owner share
  one Energy balance.
- Making creatives costs credits, not Energy. Assistants like Claude or ChatGPT connected over MCP
  spend credits but not Energy.

## Running out

- **Credits:** generating fails with "Insufficient AI generation credits. You need X but have Y."
  The owner sees an upgrade window ("Out of Generations", even when it's AI credits). Paid plans can
  buy more on Billing ("+ Buy More"); the free plan can only upgrade.
- **Plan:** UGC and Photoshoot videos on Starter or below: "UGC Video is available on the Growth and
  Enterprise plans. Upgrade to unlock it."
- **Brands:** "Maximum workspaces reached".
- Monthly credits renew with the plan; whether unused monthly credits roll over isn't something you
  can confirm. Top-ups don't expire.

## Billing (`/billing`, profile menu → Billing)

- **Free accounts** see the plans with "Get Started" (Monthly or Yearly) and an Extension Only
  option.
- **Paid accounts** see their plan, renewal date and a Credits card with "+ Buy More" for each
  balance. Upgrading, downgrading, cancelling, invoices and the card on file are all in "Manage
  Subscription" (the payment portal). A cancelled plan keeps working until the period ends.
- **Bought through Shopify:** managed in Shopify admin → Apps → Design Instantly → Manage plan.
- **AppSumo lifetime deals:** a Starter, Growth or Enterprise plan that never renews; changes and
  refunds happen on AppSumo.
- **Older (legacy) plans:** a "Grandfathered Plan" card; changing plan moves them to the new plans.
- Alerts appear for a failed payment ("Payment past due" with "Update Payment Method"), a
  cancelled, expired or revoked plan.
- Anything billing-related you can't answer: Support.
